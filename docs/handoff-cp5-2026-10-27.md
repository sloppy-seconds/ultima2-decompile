## 2. The single mistake that caused three "blockers"

**The asm tree has two numbering spaces, `label = file + 0x200`, and notes kept quoting
labels as if they were file addresses.**

* `0x1240` "sits in a gap, no file begins there" → it is `L_001240` = file `0x1040`, and
  `asm/1040_*.asm` has existed all along. It is a Minax proximity check, not the attack.
* "the caller of `asm/0C33..0C53` is undiscovered" → the search grepped `L_000c33`, which
  is `asm/0A33_cmd_done`, jumped to by 59 handlers. The tick's label is `L_000e33`.
* `asm/0AD3`'s own header cited "the `cmp $0x40` at file 0x1244 in `asm/0FCD`" and
  concluded `0x40` was the empty sentinel. `L_001244` is file `0x1044`, in `asm/1040`;
  `asm/0FCD`'s `cmp $0x40` compares a random byte; and the empty sentinel is `0x00`
  (`asm/0A74`: `or %al,%al; je next`). That header is corrected in place.

**Before declaring an address a gap, apply the bias and look again.** The generic version
of this lesson is the first entry in `docs/harness-portability.md` § 6.

## 3. Proven today — use it, do not re-derive it

**The creature turn.** Two passes over 32 slots, both in `ai.c`:
* `asm/0A6D..0AB5` per slot: skip `mon_type == 0`; raw pursuit vector `DS:19/1A =
  player - creature` (`asm/4F36`); if `|dx| < 3 && |dy| < 3` roll once in 8
  (`asm/0AAA`) and dispatch the four specials by type (`asm/0AD3`).
* `asm/2122..2260`: regen +1 below 16 HP; then try diagonal, then y-only (only if
  `dy != 0`), then x-only. Each candidate must pass `asm/2199` (refuse everything while
  `DS:26A != 0`) and the per-species tile rule: sea monster `0x2c` and frigate `0x48`
  accept water only; everything else refuses `0x10`, `0x04`, `0x00`, accepts `< 0x14`,
  and accepts `0x5c` and `0x70`. `DS:1F` is the candidate **tile byte**, not a rate.
* The step is a **unit vector**, not the distance: `asm/4F11` writes
  `sign(4 * (player - creature))`, and the ×4 puts bit 6 in the sign bit, which on a
  64-wide torus means "the shorter way is backwards". Creatures never walk the long way
  round. The measured 2-cell shift is the diagonal.
* **Contact with the player produces no attack.** The creature does not step (`asm/2201` →
  `asm/218b`), and a thief in that position steals (`asm/0B4D` → `asm/0FCD`).

**`mon_role` is the behaviour selector** (`asm/102B/1033/1035/1039`): `0` inert, bit 7
(`0x81..0x85`, the scripted townsfolk) inert, `1` the full pass, `2` move-only with no
gate and **no retreat test**, `>= 3` the undecoded wander at `asm/1086`. Most of the
measured stillness is this, not the tile rule: `monx21` ships 24 of 32 slots at role 3.

**Retreat and regen are different thresholds.** Regen at `hp < 16` (`asm/2126`), flee at
`hp < 15` (`asm/0B07`). A creature on exactly 15 heals and keeps coming. The wiki claim
that started this whole search is now bytes.

**Death** is `asm/0C62..0C99` as one span: nine zero stores into `DS:51..59`, repaint,
three newlines, the name from `DS:36` via `asm/507A`, `" IS DEAD!"`, newline. The
captured frame `captures/spice86/cp5-weak-fight/death1.png` shows it on **row 22 with
row 23 blank** — its own trailing newline scrolls it up — and all four status fields
repainted even though the routine calls only the H.P. line.

**Sites.** `E` is the door. Gate = `tile >> 2`: 5 village(digit 1), 6 town(2), 7
tower(4), 8 castle(3), 9 dungeon(5). Entry is six steps, identical in every arm:
announce, save the entry pair `DS:5A/5B`, store the sector, arrive at `(31,62)` on a
street or `(5,5)` in the first-person sectors, set the digit, load `mapx`+`monx`,
`DS:12 = 4`. Leaving is the **unmasked** edge test `asm/242D/2434`: `>= 0x40` restores
the entry pair and sets the digit to 0 — so east and south leave, west and north wrap.
Verified: all 21 gate cells in the eight world sectors name sector files that exist, and
`mapx30`'s dungeon gate is the cell the earlier live capture walked into for 3,0 → 3,5.

**Damage to the player** is `asm/0BD1..0C01`: the mask at `DS:22` is zero-tested
(`0` means no damage at all, not 1), a random byte is drawn, and the amount actually
taken is **`((rand & [DS:22]) & 0x77) + 1`** — so `DS:22` is a cap the attacker sets and
the damage is random up to it, not a fixed number. Death on underflow, then the
site/world branch that reaches the status tick. A first reading of this header said
`([DS:22] & 0x77) + 1` and was wrong; the `rand` sits between the entry test and the
mask, which is also why the site was hard to see.

**The alarm** is `asm/113A`, called from `asm/2BE9` three bytes before the `--HIT!!!`
printer: inside a site, slots 7..0 take role 1. A miss alerts nothing.

## 4. Open, in the order that pays

1. **What writes `DS:22` for the plain melee types?** The apply site is
   `asm/0BD1..0C01`, reached by fallthrough and by no label reference at all. Known
   writers of the mask: `asm/0F12` (magic missile), `asm/38B0_cmd_L` (five sites),
   `asm/0B9D` (inside the creature loop, immediately before the site branch at
   `asm/0BA0`), and `asm/0C09` (the sibling arm). The melee types named in `asm/0AD3`'s
   header (`0x30`, `0xF4`, `0xFC`) have no traced write yet, and the measured
   `9999 → 9796` is still unexplained. `asm/0B9D` is the most promising lead and is
   three instructions long.
2. **`asm/1086` — the role-3 wander.** 10–24 slots per sector. The engine leaves them
   frozen, which under-moves rather than invents. It reads a per-slot pair at
   `DS:1F7/DS:217`; those two arrays are still inside `reserved_1F7` in `state.h` and
   nobody has established whether they hold deltas or staged coordinates (both readings
   appear in `asm/1151`'s tail).
3. **How many times does one turn reach the tick?** A `CPU_EXECUTION_ADDRESS` breakpoint
   on `L_000e33` counting hits per accepted command settles W7's cadence. Agent-drivable.
4. **G27** — what the game does after the death announcement. Agent-drivable, ~20 min,
   recipe is in `docs/needs-gameplay.md`.
5. **G28** — the `DS:14` value at a gate cell. Now much cheaper than the ticket says: the
   gate table is proven from the map files, so G28 only needs to confirm that
   `[DS:14] == tile >> 1` at one gate, not sweep for the gate itself.
6. The owner playing a fight and a town in the SDL build.

## 5. Traps newly paid for

* **Two address spaces.** See § 2. It cost three false blockers.
* **A search that greps for a subtract naming a field misses every site that loads the
  operand from scratch first.** `asm/0BDB` was missed twice for exactly this.
* **`--play-map`'s `player_x` in `--dump-state` is `DS:23`, the tile-probe scratch, not
  the player's position.** The position is `DS:00/01` and is not in the dump. Reading
  that field as "where am I" will mislead anyone driving the fight scripts.
* **The engine's grid is stamped, so `u2_world_tile_at` returns a creature's type byte
  for an occupied cell.** That is faithful (the original stamps too) and it is what makes
  creatures block each other for free — but it also means a site transition must clear the
  six creature arrays before loading, or the old sector's creatures survive into the new
  one. `site_reload` does this.
* **`make test` compiles some harnesses from explicit source lists**, so adding a file
  under `src/game/` means editing the lists in `run_cp4.sh` too. `ai.c` was added to both.
