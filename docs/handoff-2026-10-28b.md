The owner drove the noVNC build (`deploy/novnc`, `http://ai.lan:6081/vnc.html`) and reported two
behavioural bugs and one design fact. Both bugs were real, both came from a reading the engine
had inherited, and in both cases the original's behaviour was **already in the capture corpus**:

contradicts the model earns a re-derivation of the model before it earns a defence of it.
