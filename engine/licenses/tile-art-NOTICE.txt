This is recorded here although it is not third-party work, because it is the one
asset whose authorship could reasonably be questioned by someone reading a
reconstruction of a commercial game, and the answer is a procedure rather than an
assertion.

It was made under a cleanroom wall: a spec author who had examined the original
binary wrote intent-only requirements — what a tile must communicate, never pixel
coordinates, measured densities or component sizes. A separate implementer drew
the art from that specification alone, in a tree containing no game art. The
result communicates the same things as the original CGA tiles. It is not a copy
of them.

Full procedure, verification chain, build history and the recorded failures:
`docs/cleanroom-procedure.md`. The legal record that travels with the assets:
`engine/assets/tiles/cleanroom/PROVENANCE.md`. The specification they were drawn
from: `docs/tile-art-spec.md`.
