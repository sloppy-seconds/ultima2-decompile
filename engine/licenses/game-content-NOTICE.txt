About the game itself — what this binary does and does not contain
=================================================================

Ultima II is a commercial game, copyright Origin Systems. This project is a
reconstruction of its engine, and the distinction matters for what a shipped
binary actually carries:

NOT embedded, and never distributed by this project
  * The game's art and sound. Tiles are original substitute art drawn under a
    cleanroom procedure (see the tile-art notice in this output). The PC-speaker
    tones are generated from the PIT divisor the original latched, not sampled.
  * The world data: sector maps, monster placements, NPC dialogue, pictures.
    These are read at runtime from a directory the player supplies with
    --data-dir, and they are never committed to the repository or shipped in a
    binary.
  * The original executable.

Embedded in this binary
  * Short functional text strings the engine must print — menu labels, prompts,
    item and spell names — extracted from the game executable by
    engine/tools/extract/extractor.py. There is no cleanroom path for these: if
    the engine prints anything other than what the original printed, it is
    behaviourally wrong. They are generated into a committed header,
    engine/content/gen_content.h, and the extractor is that file's only writer.
  * Our own reconstruction of the original program's behaviour, as C.
